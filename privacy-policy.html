Privacy Policy

SUBWORTH / Last updated: September 3, 2026

日本語

The short version: this app collects nothing.
Everything you enter is stored on your device and is never sent anywhere, including to the developer.

Information we collect

None. The app does not do any of the following.

  • Collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal information
  • Require an account or sign-in
  • Track usage or analytics (no analytics SDK is included)
  • Serve ads or include an advertising SDK
  • Access your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
  • Connect to your bank, credit card, or email to detect subscriptions
  • Read what you do in other apps

How your data is stored

Your subscriptions, the days you marked as used, your review decisions, and your cancellation history are stored only on your device, in a local database. Nothing is sent to a server. The developer has no way to see any of it.

Deleting the app deletes this data from your device. There is currently no backup and no sync between devices.

Notifications

Reminders about upcoming charges and free-trial endings are local notifications built on your device. Their contents are never sent anywhere. Notifications are off by default and can be turned off at any time in the app's settings.

In-app purchase

Purchases are handled by Apple (the App Store). Apple handles all payment information; the developer never receives your credit card details or anything that identifies you. The app only receives whether the purchase has been made.

Sharing with third parties

There is no collected information, so there is nothing to share.

Children's privacy

The app can be used at any age. Because it does not collect personal information at all, it does not collect information from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page is updated along with the date above. If a future version were to start collecting information, that change would be announced with the app update itself.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the contact address on the support page.
